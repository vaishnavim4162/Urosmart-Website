Medical Disclaimer

Please read carefully before use

⚠️ For Informational Purposes Only

UroSmart is a clinical decision-support aid intended to help trained medical professionals interpret urinalysis microscopy images. It is NOT a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. The analysis results displayed by this app must not be used as the sole basis for any medical decision.

1. No Medical Advice

The content provided by UroSmart — including text, AI-generated analysis results, bounding box detections, and PDF reports — is for informational and educational purposes only. It does not constitute medical advice and should not be relied upon as such.

2. Consult a Healthcare Professional

Always seek the advice of your physician, radiologist, or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition. Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking it because of something you have read or seen in this application.

3. No Doctor-Patient Relationship

Use of UroSmart does not establish a doctor-patient relationship between you and the developers, SIMATS, or any affiliated institution.

4. Accuracy Limitations

The AI detection model provides probabilistic results and may produce false positives or false negatives. Results are dependent on image quality, sample preparation, and microscope settings. All results must be verified by a qualified laboratory technician or physician before clinical use.

5. Regulatory Status

UroSmart is intended for use by trained healthcare professionals in conjunction with standard clinical practice. It is not approved as a standalone diagnostic device. Regulatory clearance documentation has been submitted to Apple App Review.

6. Emergency Situations

If you believe a patient is experiencing a medical emergency, call emergency services immediately. Do not rely on this app in emergency situations.

7. Contact

For regulatory or medical questions, please contact SIMATS at medical@simats.ac.in

By using UroSmart you acknowledge that you have read and understood this disclaimer, that you are a trained healthcare professional, and that this app is to be used as a decision-support tool only.
